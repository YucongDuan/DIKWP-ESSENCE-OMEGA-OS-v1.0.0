# Ultimate Without Finalism

“Ultimate” is used here as a design demand, not as an achieved metaphysical title. The runtime reaches toward the deepest available causal-generative invariant while making four refusals:

1. no local success becomes a universal theorem without a quantified bridge;
2. no functional continuity becomes phenomenal consciousness without independent evidence;
3. no causal efficacy becomes legitimate authority without named permission;
4. no compact description may erase residual harm, provenance, refusal, or incompatible worlds.

The terminal output is therefore not a final noun. It is a package:

\[
(\text{local essence},\text{scope},\text{counterexamples},\text{residual frontier},\text{next probe},\text{responsible owner}).
\]

An open residual is not an embarrassment. It is the mathematical address of what the current system cannot yet regenerate.
