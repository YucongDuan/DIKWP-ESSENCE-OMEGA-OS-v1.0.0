# Source and Independence Boundary

## Source-derived foundations

The runtime inherits from Yucong Duan and Feng Wang's *A New Mathematical History of the Universe* the following methodological commitments: records before concept aliases; multiple D/I/K/W/P records; all twenty-five directed role routes; append-only lineage; operational W; executable forward and reverse P; explicit sameness after regeneration; local worlds; future-effect grouping; and preservation of open residuals.

The article *Constructing a Cognitive State Equation* by Li Deyi, Yin Jialun, Di Kaichang, and Liu Yuchao is treated as an important candidate world model. Its dissipative-system, reaction-diffusion, free-energy, memory-first, and cognitive-field commitments may be entered as hypotheses and tested. They do not govern this runtime as fixed first principles.
