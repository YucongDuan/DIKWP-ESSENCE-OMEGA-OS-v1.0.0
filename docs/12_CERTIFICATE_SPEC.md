# Bounded Essence Certificate Specification

A certificate records:

- system and version;
- scenario and declared scope;
- purpose owner;
- selected candidate identities and component sets;
- essence level and metrics;
- predeclared falsification conditions;
- prohibited inferences;
- non-aggregated status vector;
- residual frontier;
- probe plan;
- semantic graph and essence-lattice digests;
- evidence-ledger head;
- causal handoff;
- certificate identifier and SHA-256 digest.

The certificate is invalid as evidence for any claim outside its declared scope. It is also invalid if the scenario, code, or ledger has been changed without producing a successor certificate.
