# Non-Annexing Essence Federation

Certificate federation compares bounded results from different subjects, institutions, carriers, or experiments. It computes:

- the intersection of selected component sets;
- the union of components;
- local-only components;
- the union of open residual types;
- combined prohibited inferences.

The intersection is not called the final shared essence. A component name may itself require a cross-carrier mapping, and empty intersection does not imply incompatible worlds. Federation therefore produces a comparison object and successor questions, not a sovereign ontology.
